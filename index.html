Who We Are

An institutional mandate for lasting transformation.

Manus Dei Global Initiative for Transformation (MDGIFT) is an institutional organization with a transgenerational mandate to bring about transformation and revival, bringing spiritual, personal, and societal transformation and revival by aligning lives, systems, and structures with God's divine order.

Anchored on the scriptural foundation of Exodus 25:8–9, MDGIFT seeks to build according to God's blueprint, restore order, and raise a Kingdom standard through diverse initiatives.

“Have the people of Israel build me a holy sanctuary so I can live among them. You must build this Tabernacle and its furnishings exactly like the pattern I will show you.”
Exodus 25:8–9, NLT

The MDGIFT Framework

One foundation. Three anchors.

Our mandate, mission and vision provide a shared direction for every expression of Manus Dei Global Initiative for Transformation.

01 / Mandate

Transformation & Revival

To bring about spiritual, personal, and societal transformation and revival by aligning lives, systems, and structures with God's divine order.

02 / Mission

Divine Service to Humanity

To serve humanity through initiatives that communicate the love, truth, presence, and purposes of God, providing spiritual, social, practical, and transformational solutions to human needs.

03 / Vision

Order, Revival & the Redemption of Humanity

To see humanity restored to God's divine order, awakened through revival, and brought into the fullness of His purpose, resulting in the redemption and transformation of individuals, communities, systems, and society.

Our Initiatives

Many expressions. One divine direction.

MDGIFT advances its mandate through diverse initiatives designed to address different dimensions of spiritual, human, social, and societal transformation.

Manus Dei Christian Centre

“Building According to Pattern”

A nurturing centre committed to transforming lives and reviving souls through discipleship, ministry, training, consultation, fellowship, and practical expressions of the Christian faith.

Arms include:

  • Manus Dei Clinic
  • Manus Dei Ministry
  • Manus Dei United
  • Manus Dei Kingdom Training Institute
  • Manus Dei Foundation

Project I CAN

“A Simple CAN Can Make a Change.”

A micro-giving initiative that turns small contributions into immediate acts of compassion for people in need. It demonstrates how small acts of generosity can become meaningful expressions of love, care, and practical support.

Mission Hope & Light

“Tripartite Prosperity”

A Christ-centred initiative awakening lives through the light of Christ and guiding individuals into true prosperity, beginning with the soul and extending into every area of life. Transformation begins with salvation and impacts the spirit, soul, and body.

BRiDGE

“Bridging the Gap Between the Church and the Unchurched”

An outreach initiative taking the Gospel beyond traditional church settings and into the lives of those yet to encounter its truth, through intentional presence, practical support, and compassionate engagement.

Bank of Welfare

“Relief – Stability – Sustainable”

A community-driven initiative focused on relief and welfare, building pathways from short-term assistance towards stability and sustainability.

From crisis to capability.
From dependence to contribution.

Priesthood Assembly

“Called. Chosen. Consecrated.”

A sacred gathering of the called and chosen, rooted in 1 Peter 2:9 and Revelation 5:10, consecrated as vessels to incubate God's glory, carry His presence, and advance His Kingdom.

The Heart of the Work

Purpose made practical. Transformation made tangible.

MDGIFT brings spiritual conviction into meaningful service: formation, outreach, compassion, welfare, and the renewal of individuals, communities, systems, and society.
